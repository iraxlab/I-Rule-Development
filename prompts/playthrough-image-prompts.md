## Island 1: Arrival

### PT-01 The World Is a Climb
*Beat: map (island map, elevation by stage). Attach: PT-01 layout guide (art/guide/layout-guide.png), key-art references: Leap, Flying, The Throne, First of Many Fires (art/key-art/13, 14, 16, 18), island map page 1 (design/Island_Map.pdf), Fire Eye reference (art/key-art/10-the-fire-eye-reference.jpg), ENV-01 hero view (art/assets/ENV-01/hero.jpg) and blueprint sheets (art/guide/env01-blueprint-v2-A-plan.png, env01-blueprint-v2-B-section.png), ENV-02 sheet (Stepping Island), ENV-03 sheet and detail 1 (stone arch), ENV-04 sheet (Large Plateau), ENV-05 sheet (Causeway), ENV-06 sheet (Arena), ENV-08 sheet (Canyon Pair), ENV-07 sheet (Summit Peak), six-lines key art, THR-01 Throne sheet.*

```
Use the attached reference images as strict visual references: keep the exact same island, beam, door, character face and suit, and art style, and change only the camera and action. A single cinematic 16:9 widescreen storyboard frame, photoreal game-cinematic concept art. Extreme wide view from just behind and above the Starting Shelf in the south-west, looking north-east up the whole climb, exactly as in the layout guide. Use the attached LAYOUT GUIDE image as the exact composition: same camera, island positions, sizes, spacing and heights, same numbers, and repaint it in the exact look of the attached key-art references (The Leap, Flying, The Throne, The First of Many Fires): warm gold-amber cinematic dusk, dark bronze-lit stone, a sea of clouds, photoreal. THE FEELING: this is a world far too big and too complex to finish in an hour, and the goal is not visible yet. It is a SHATTERED WORLD, not a path: a whole planet was blown apart and the ten numbered islands are only the biggest surviving pieces. Fill the frame with wreckage at every depth: torn rock shards and slabs in the foreground, colossal broken crust plates hanging far behind, a few shards cracked with molten amber. The numbered islands stay the largest and best lit so a first climb can be read, but the route is long, indirect and mostly unseen. THE GOAL IS HIDDEN: the Throne spire at the top left is almost lost in layers of haze and distant shards, only a faint thin dark spire with a tiny amber glint, so the player cannot yet tell where the climb ends. Every island carries tall dark ruined bronze-lit towers and spires, as in the references: tapering tower ruins with sheared tops on the plateau (5), capped drum towers and one needle spire on the arena (7), twin towers with a broken arch on the canyon (8), a gatehouse tower on the summit stair, with glowing amber lanterns on the tallest. Where there is a gap between islands, the crossing is a RUNG BRIDGE: two chains with glowing amber horizontal bars as rungs, exactly like the glowing bars of the six lines, as in The Leap reference; some gaps have no bridge at all. Not a plank-and-rope bridge. In the foreground, step 1, the dark cracked Starting Shelf with its bronze ring disc and two lantern posts, very large in the lower frame, and the tiny Elias with the Cinder at its edge looking up. The Fire Eye is HUGE: it fills the top of the frame, copied from the attached Fire Eye reference: an almond eye of living fire, white-gold at the lid and orange-amber across a radial-fibre iris, a dark vertical diamond pupil, long jagged flame filaments streaming far out from its outline. Clearly BELOW the Eye and never hidden by it, the six sky lines hang large and separate as six long glowing amber bars, each one wider than the biggest island, as in the Summit reference. The RING OF FIRE: a vast thin ring of dormant fire encircles the whole world far away on the horizon, a line of smouldering embers and low dark flames curving around everything, so its enormous size shows. It is not yet ignited, it only smoulders, and it is the proof of how large the world is. The Fire Dragon is MASSIVE and unmissable, as in the Flying reference: a huge winged dragon with dark bronze-black scales, wings spread wide and veined with molten amber, banking in side view across the upper middle of the frame, trailing sparks. Two LARGE planets fill the sky, as in the references: a huge ringed planet and a huge cracked glowing ember planet, both partly hazed. Warm low sun, long rim light on the towers, layered haze so each island is hazier than the last. Only the labels listed below. Soft gold-amber haze, warm light on the high spire, the lowest tiers in cooler shadow. Design notes: the world is NOT flat: it is a six-tier climb of broken floating islands at rising heights. The Starting Shelf is the lowest island at the lower left. Beside it a small twisted stepping island and a medium island with a windbreak ridge. Then a field of drifting moving platforms rising to a large plateau, a circular arena to the east, a canyon cliff pair far to the north-east, a drifting cluster of fragments across the north, and the highest point of all, a slender summit spire crowned by the Throne at the upper left. Every tier sits higher than the last, with cloud seas below and between them. The distances are vast: the Throne is about 700 m from the start, roughly seventeen widths of the Starting Shelf, and about 360 m higher, so from island 1 it is a tiny spire at the far horizon with ten islands and wide gaps of cloud and void between; the gaps are as wide as or wider than the islands, the bridges are long thin threads and some gaps have no bridge at all, only drifting slabs; the Starting Shelf Island exactly as in the attached approved ENV-01 hero image and blueprint sheets: a ragged floating shelf of dark rock, an irregular cracked spawn terrace with the sunken bronze arrival disc where the player appears, a broken worn road to a crooked weathered bronze beam lying across a jagged meandering crack, a broken forecourt and three worn steps to the locked door cut into a huge black cliff with one enormous deep mountain of boulder rock (36 m shoulder, 60 m summit, two high peaks of the same mass) of the same dark rock growing out of it, no masonry, thick rusted chains hanging from bronze rim rings, bare dead trees and dry grass; the six sky lines, six long straight horizontal glowing bars, evenly spaced and parallel, stacked like a ladder, never stars or circles; the Throne, about 40 m tall, standing on top of a slender summit spire island (about 50 m wide and 40 m tall, ringed by terraces and wound by one long carved staircase) at the very highest point of the world, hewn from black volcanic stone and bronze, a deep seat with two thick armrests, a very tall narrow monolith backrest with six equally spaced horizontal fissures, two colossal curved bronze dragon ribs arching inward on both sides, molten-amber cracks, a long steep staircase of broken black stone with bronze braziers on both sides, never modern, glossy or floating. Labelled storyboard: add small clean engraved-bronze callout labels with thin amber leader lines, each pointing at its subject and reading exactly: "1 Starting Shelf (start)", "2 Stepping Island", "3 Medium Island: Line 1", "4 Moving Platforms", "5 Large Plateau: Line 2", "6 Causeway", "7 Arena: Line 3", "8 Canyon: Line 4", "9 Fragments: Line 5", "10 Summit and Throne: Line 6", "Six Lines", "Fire Eye", "Fire Dragon", "Ring of Fire". Also a small location tag in the top-left corner reading exactly "Island 1: Arrival" and a small action tag in the top-right corner reading exactly "The World Is a Climb". Use ONLY these labels and add no other labels or text anywhere (do not name or label any other island or object). Each label sits beside its subject with the leader line ending exactly on it, is short, high-contrast, spelled correctly, appears once and never covers the main subject. Layout: a thin black header strip along the top edge holds the location tag (left) and action tag (right); a thin black footer strip along the bottom holds the caption; the footer strip reads exactly "PT-01 - THE WORLD IS A CLIMB", appears only once; no other text, no UI, no HUD, no logos. Match the approved key art: ancient weathered dark volcanic stone and aged bronze, weathered sandstone-tan and dark charcoal-brown layered rock with obsidian-black shards, warm gold-amber dusty haze, ember glow, photoreal and cinematic, severe and mythic, closer to Dune than to fantasy spectacle, calm monumental mood. All islands belong to one family: the same layered sandstone-tan and dark charcoal-brown rock with obsidian-black strata, the same aged-bronze trim, the same dry thin grass and the same tapering hanging undersides; they differ in shape and size, never in material or colour. Broken floating islands and ruins only, NOT a city. No skyscrapers, no glass towers, no neon, no modern architecture, no lush green vegetation, no water, no crowds, no gore, no religious imagery, no face on the sky eye.
```
