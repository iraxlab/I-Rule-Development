## If text is still unreadable after this
Some generators cap how much text they can render cleanly no matter what the prompt says. If that's what's happening in Freepik specifically:
1. Generate each page with **no text at all** — just the scene, badge shape empty, title/subtitle area left blank.
2. Add the badge label, title, and subtitle yourself afterward in a simple layer — Canva, Photoshop, or even PowerPoint — using a real bold font at a size you control directly.
This guarantees legibility regardless of what the image model does, and it's genuinely how most professional key-art decks are made — the art and the typography are usually two separate passes, not one.
