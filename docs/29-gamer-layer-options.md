# The gamer layer: other options for the developers

> **What this is:** a menu of other ways to build each part of [the gamer layer](28-gamer-layer.md). The **chosen** option is the one the producer approved. The others are here in case the developers find one more fun after a playtest.
>
> **Rules for every option:** no HUD, no text, no score popups, no skill tree, no shop. The world answers the player.
>
> **STATUS:** options only. Nothing here is decided. Change a chosen option only after a playtest and with the producer's OK.

**How to read the tables:** *Like* is a game that does something similar. *Watch out* is the risk.

---

## 1. Fun: the Chord

**Chosen: the Chord.** Clean moves add notes, up to six.

| Option | What it is | Like | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chosen: the Chord** | Each clean move adds a note. A mistake drops one. Six notes is Beauty. | Rez, Sayonara Wild Hearts | The mix must stay beautiful when notes drop |
| Flow ribbon | A glowing ribbon trails Elias. It grows with clean moves and frays when he rushes. | Tony Hawk combo, Sonic | Easy to see, but it is a bit like a meter |
| Breath rhythm | The world has a slow breath. Moving in time with it gives a small boost. | Journey, Abzu | Hard to feel at first. Needs a clear sound cue |
| Wind store | Clean moves gather wind that gives one longer dash. | The Pathless | It becomes a resource to manage |

## 2. Fun: the bronze rail

**Chosen: the bronze line gives a small boost when run with rhythm.**

| Option | What it is | Like | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chosen: the rail boost** | A steady rhythm on the bronze line gives a smooth speed-up. | Sonic Frontiers, The Pathless | None found |
| Heat wake | The ground warms where he walked calmly and cools where he rushed. | Death Stranding footprints | Needs a ground effect on every island |
| Echo steps | Stones ring in a scale when he steps on the beat. | Rhythm games | Can feel like a music game |
| Grind line | The bronze line is a rail he slides along. | Sonic, Jet Set Radio | Too much speed. It fights the stillness idea |

## 3. Fun: the feel of an action (juice)

**Chosen: a short freeze on an open-frame hit, a slow-down on a perfect parry, a bell and a chime.**

| Option | What it is | Like | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chosen: freeze and slow-down** | 0.06 s freeze on a hit, 0.15 s slow-down on a parry. | Sekiro, Sifu | Keep it short or it feels laggy |
| Shimmer | A soft ripple crosses the screen on a perfect action. | Ghost of Tsushima | Hard to see in a bright scene |
| Controller pulse | A soft heartbeat in the controller when calm, a buzz when rushing. | PS5 and Xbox games | Not every player has haptics |
| Silent beat | The whole sound drops out for 0.2 s, then returns bright. | Hotline Miami | Could clash with the calm mood |

## 4. Game Loop: the first rush

**Chosen: the rusty beam on island 1 shakes if he sprints.**

| Option | What it is | Like | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chosen: the rusty beam** | Sprint and the beam shakes and dust falls. | Uncharted | The beam is already in the canon |
| Loose bridge | A plank bridge sways if he rushes. | Zelda, Tomb Raider | Needs a bridge on island 1 |
| Hot stone path | The floor hums and glows hot if he rushes. | Journey sand | Needs a ground effect |
| Shy Cinder | The Cinder flies away from him if he rushes. It returns when he stops. | The Last Guardian | It could feel like the game is angry at him |

## 5. Curiosity: the Still Secrets

**Chosen: a seam opens, one lantern lights and burns gold in the ending.**

**The secret itself**
| Option | What it is | Like | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chosen: the seam** | Stand still 3 s. A seam in the rock glows and opens. | Zelda shrine hum | The tone must be quiet but findable |
| Echo figure | A faint still figure stands where the secret is. Stand beside it and it opens. | Journey, Dark Souls ghosts | It could read as a character. Keep it faceless |
| Cinder hint | The Cinder drifts toward the secret only when he is calm. | Pathless, Last Guardian | Might make it too easy |
| Wind whisper | The wind carries a faint note from the secret. Follow the sound. | Hellblade | Needs good headphones |

**The reward**
| Option | What it is | Like | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|

| Sky constellation | The lanterns he lit form a shape in the sky at the end. | Outer Wilds | Needs a new sky effect |

**The big reward for all seven**
| Option | What it is | Like | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|

## 6. Skill: the Mastery layer

**Flawless wave**
| Option | What it is | Like | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chosen: enemies go passive, the Dragon flies lower** | Hold the Chord at four notes through a wave. | Bayonetta witch time | Players must notice it |
| Bow and fade | Enemies bow and fade, not shatter. | Sekiro, Ghost of Tsushima | Needs a new death animation |
| Ember gift | The Cinder drops an Ember Capsule. | Many games | It adds an item, and the game has almost none |

**The big arrow**
| Option | What it is | Like | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chosen: resonance strike** | Open-frame hit plus a parry within 4 s makes a one-shot arrow. | Sekiro, Sifu | Already in the canon |
| Still arrow | Hold the bow still for 1.5 s. The arrow pierces two enemies. | Ghost of Tsushima standoff | Slow in a fight |
| Echo arrow | A perfect dodge makes the next arrow split in two. | Devil May Cry | A bit like aggression |

**The Calm Score**
| Option | What it is | Like | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chosen: Fire Eye glow and a Steam stat** | A glow after the ending and a private stat. No leaderboard. | Journey | Needs a stat |
| Ghost run | Your best calm run shows as a faint ghost. | Racing games | Needs recording |
| Daily Quiet | A daily seed. Everyone plays the same storm. | Spelunky | Needs a server |

**The second run**
| Option | What it is | Like | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|

## 9. The seven decisions: other options

**9.1 The fall rule for the arena.** Chosen: A, the Cinder catch. The full A to E list is in [doc 25](25-env06-blueprint.md). Two more:

| Option | What it is | Like | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cinder catch with a cost | The catch works, but the Cinder is dim for 5 s. | Prince of Persia, Hades | It punishes a little |
| Hidden lip | A rock lip under each broken edge lets him jump back up. | Celeste, Mario | Needs a rock lip on Sheet B |

**9.2 The ENV-01 ledge fight.** Chosen: a gentle bow lesson, 2 weak enemies.

| Option | What it is | Like | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chosen: gentle lesson** | 2 weak enemies, one opening each, no splitting. | Zelda first fight | None found |

| No fight | Birds and leeches only fly past. He crosses safely. | Journey | Loses the bow reward |
| Bridge fight | The fight happens on the stone bridge, not the ledge. | Uncharted | More risk of a fall |

**9.3 The Shadow fight.** Chosen: no fight on island 1. A faint echo at the Summit.

| Option | What it is | Like | Watch out |

| Mirror puzzle | On island 1 a glass copy mirrors his moves. Stand still and it fades. | Braid | Needs animation work |
| Short film (the old plan) | The duel on island 1 with three floating weapons and a ring. | Old long playthrough | It looks like a boss. Does not match the canon |
| Knight echo | The echo appears at the Burn Knight in stage 3. | Dark Souls | Mixes it with a fight |

**9.4 Leeches on ENV-05.** Chosen: none.

| Option | What it is | Like | Watch out |

| **Chosen: none** | A calm walk with wind and spire pairs. | Journey | Could be too quiet |

| Option | What it is | Like | Watch out |

| Option | What it is | Like | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|

## 10. How to pick another option

1. Playtest the chosen option first.
2. Ask the playtest question: "When did you realize you had to stop moving?"
3. If players feel bored, lost or frustrated, try the next option in the table.
4. Test one change at a time.
5. Write the result in [Canon and open decisions](13-canon-and-open-decisions.md).
