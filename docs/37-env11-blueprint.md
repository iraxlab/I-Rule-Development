# ENV-11 Moving Platform Slab: Blueprint (Stage 2)

Sheets: [A approach chain](../art/guide/env11-blueprint-A-top.png) · [B side and timing](../art/guide/env11-blueprint-B-side.png) · [C the slab](../art/guide/env11-blueprint-C-slab.png)

## 1. What it is
One floating slab of natural dark rock, used eight times in Stage 2: four in the approach chain from ENV-03 to ENV-04 and four across the ENV-04 crater. Each glides back and forth along its own short route and waits at each end. **Nothing is built:** no piston, no rail, no metal rim, no chains, no handles. It floats by itself like every other rock.

## 2. The slab
8 m wide, 5.5 m deep, flat cracked top, a thick rough chipped rim about 1.5 m thick and a short tapering root about 2.5 m deep. Dark black-brown fractured rock with only thin lighter brown seams, like the Starting Shelf Island. Two shallow parallel grooves with a faint amber glow run along the top in the direction of travel. The glow pulses once as the slab passes.

## 3. The route, shown to the player
A faint thin line of amber light in the air along the route, with a bronze anchor socket (STR-09) set flush in the rock at each end of the route. That is the only bronze. No arrows, no text.

## 4. Approach chain (sheet A and B)
| Slab | Route (blueprint x) | Height |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | 20 to 34 | +6.8 m |
| P2 | 46 to 60 | +7.6 m |
| P3 | 72 to 86 | +8.4 m |
| P4 | 98 to 112 | +9.2 m |
The 12 m stone bridge from ENV-03 ends at +6 m. The ENV-04 west landing is at +10 m. Each route is 14 m long, slab 8 m wide, so the gap between slabs is 4 m at the closest and up to 26 m at the widest.

## 5. Timing (sheet B)
Glide 14 m at 1.5 m/s (9.3 s), wait 2 s at each end: a full cycle of about 22.6 s. Neighbours start a quarter cycle apart (5.5 s). A low tone plays at each end. Stillness does not change the cycle; it makes the tone clearer so the timing is easy to hear. Dashing twice in 3 s raises the dash cooldown to 1.4 s.

## 6. Crater crossing (ENV-04)
Four slabs of the same type drift across the 30 m crater, one after the other, as in [doc 22](22-env04-blueprint.md). Their routes are not drawn here; place them within the ENV-04 crater. Stage 2 therefore uses eight slabs in all.

## 7. Failure
A fall is a soft reset to the last anchor socket. No damage.

## 8. Image prompts
Sheet, five views and four details are on the ENV-11 asset page. New Gemini chat each time; attach the ENV-10 sheet and the ENV-01 hero. The prompt does not name metal, pistons or chains, because naming them makes Gemini draw them.
