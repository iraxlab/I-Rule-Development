# Island dressing guide

Which structures go on which island. The islands are bare rock plus one signature feature; towers are now **built into the island prompts** (fused to the island rock and close to its signature feature, so each island sheet shows its complete look), while the STR tower assets below stay as style references and reusable kit pieces. Lanterns, trees and other props are separate assets. Placement is a **suggestion** for level design: confirm against [03 World and level design](03-world-and-level-design.md) and the [stage plans](stages/README.md).

## The tower kit

| Asset | Form | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| STR-01 Tower Ruin, Large | Seven-tier tapering tower, sheared top | Big landmark on large islands |
| STR-02 Tower Ruin, Capped | Squat drum tower, verdigris dome, spiral stair | Squat anchor point; carries a lantern or a lookout |
| STR-03 Rock Spire Cluster | Seven black needles with a fused balcony | Natural skyline filler; breaks up flat rims |
| STR-04 Monolith Slab | Slightly leaning obsidian monolith | Quiet marker; stillness beats |
| STR-23 Leaning Broken Tower | Tower tilted mid-fall, chains and buttress | Tension and ruin; shows the world has fallen |
| STR-24 Needle Spire Tower | Impossibly slender 45 m needle tower | Far landmark you can see from several islands |
| STR-25 Twin Towers with Broken Arch | Twin towers joined by a broken arch | Pairs on big plateaus; frames a view |
| STR-26 Gatehouse Tower | Gatehouse with a huge arch passage | Entrances and mid-climb waypoints |
