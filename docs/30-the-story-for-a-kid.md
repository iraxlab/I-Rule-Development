# The story for a kid: from the first step to the Throne

## The start: who is who

1. There is a land called Igara.
2. Its islands float in the sky.
3. Long ago the fire of the land broke, because someone rushed it.
4. A boy named Elias is chosen to fix it.
5. Elias is not the strongest. He is the calmest.
6. A tiny fire friend rides with him. It is called the Cinder.
7. The Cinder always points the way.
8. High in the sky a big eye watches. It is the Fire Eye.
9. Six dark bars hang in the sky. They are the six lines.
10. Far, far above sits a giant Throne.
11. Elias must light all six lines. Then he can reach the Throne.
12. **The one rule:** if you rush, the world gets loud and angry. If you stop, the world gets calm.

**A secret to tell the kid:** when Elias moves well, soft music grows, one note at a time, up to six notes. If he panics, a note drops.

---

## Chapter 1: Island 1, the first shelf

*Goal: open the door, take the bow, leave by the bridge.*

1. Elias wakes up on a round bronze disc.
2. The Cinder floats by his shoulder.
3. A thin bronze line runs away from the disc. He follows it.
4. He comes to a rusty beam over a gap.
5. If he runs, the beam shakes and dust falls. The sound goes harsh.
6. If he walks slowly, the beam goes quiet and steady. He crosses it.
7. He climbs 3 stone steps.
8. He reaches a big locked door with chains on it.
9. He stands still in front of the door.
10. The chains fall. The doors open.
11. He walks into a dark cave.
12. He finds a glowing bow and takes it.
13. He walks out to the ledge.
14. Two small, weak bat-birds and leeches come for him.
15. He shoots them with fire arrows, one at a time, when they open up.
16. He crosses the stone bridge to the next island.

**He learns:** stand still, and the door opens.

**A hidden thing:** next to the bronze disc is a rock seam. If he stands still beside it for 3 seconds, it glows and opens. A lantern on the cliff lights.

---

## Chapter 2: Island 2, the little stepping island

*Goal: rest, then cross.*

1. Elias steps onto a tiny round pad.
2. The leeches stay behind. It is quiet.
3. A giant bird flies over, once.
4. If he stands still, it passes by.
5. He looks east and sees island 3.
6. He crosses the plank bridge.

**He learns:** when you are calm, danger passes.

**A hidden thing:** at the root of the dead tree is a rock seam. Stand still there and a lantern lights on a branch.

---

## Chapter 3: Island 3, the windy island (Line 1)

*Goal: light the first fire.*

1. The wind is loud outside.
2. Elias walks into the hollow. The wind stops.
3. He stands still for 2 seconds.
4. He turns to face the stone column.
5. He holds still for 2.5 seconds.
6. Fire lights on the column.
7. **Line 1** lights up in the sky.
8. He sits and rests on the flat rock.
9. He crosses the short bridge.

**He learns:** standing still makes things happen.

**A hidden thing:** under the rest rock is a seam. Stand still and a lantern lights on the rim.

---

## Chapter 4: the moving slabs

1. Four stone slabs float and move.
2. Elias jumps from one to the next.
3. They rise up to the next island.

---

## Chapter 5: Island 4, the big plateau (Line 2)

*Goal: light the second fire.*

1. Elias lands on the west side.
2. He sees a deep crater and a fire column.
3. Three paths lead on.
4. He stands still for 1.5 seconds.
5. The Cinder points to the middle path.
6. The wrong paths crack. He falls to a ledge. The Cinder catches him. No pain.
7. He jumps across the crater on 4 moving slabs.
8. On the crater floor he stands still. His strength comes back.
9. He climbs up to the column.
10. He holds still for 3 seconds.
11. Fire lights. **Line 2** lights up in the sky.
12. He crosses the stone bridge between the two giant rocks.

**He learns:** the easy way is not always the right way.

**A hidden thing:** low in the crater wall is a seam. Stand still and a lantern lights at the crater edge.

---

## Chapter 6: the three cracking slabs

1. Three slabs lead up to the next island.
2. Each one cracks after 2 seconds.
3. Elias keeps moving. He does not stop here.

---

## Chapter 7: Island 5, the long thin path

*Goal: walk to the end and stay calm.*

1. Elias steps on. The wind drops.
2. He walks east along the bronze line.
3. Giant rocks lean over the path.
4. At the broken rock the edge shakes.
5. He steps carefully.
6. In the calm spot he stands still for 2 seconds. The wind drops again.
7. Near the end the wind rises.
8. He crosses the chain bridge. The chains clink low and heavy.

**He learns:** calm beats panic. This is a quiet walk. There are no leeches here.

**A hidden thing:** under the middle pair of leaning rocks is a seam. Stand still and a lantern lights in the middle of the path.

---

## Chapter 8: Island 6, the arena (Line 3)

*Goal: win the first big fight.*

1. He crosses the bridge. The wind drops. It is very quiet.
2. A big round arena opens. Five vents glow.
3. **Wave 1.** Two glass shadows appear. He fights.
4. He shoots again and again, fast. The shadows split into more.
5. Dust rises. The sound turns harsh.
6. A storm comes. An edge cracks, glowing red.
7. If he falls, the Cinder catches him. He lands in the calm spot behind the tall rock.
8. He runs behind the tall rock. He stands still for 2 seconds.
9. The storm fades.
10. **Wave 2.** More glass shadows, and leeches climb up at the broken edges.
11. He waits for one to open up. He shoots once. It breaks.
12. In the middle of the fight the Fire Eye turns a little toward him.
13. **Wave 3.** A burn knight comes, with a big shield.
14. His arrows bounce off the shield.
15. He waits for the right moment and blocks. The shield drops.
16. One arrow ends the knight.
17. A fire column rises.
18. He holds still for **4 seconds**.
19. **Line 3** lights up in the sky.
20. The Dragon flies over, once. It does not attack.
21. He walks to the east gap and crosses the bridge.

**He learns:** rushing makes more enemies. Stillness ends the storm.

**A hidden thing:** after the fight, one dull vent has a seam. Stand still and a lantern lights at the east gap.

---

## Chapter 9: the canyon (Line 4)

*Goal: build bridges by standing still.*

1. The gaps are wide. There is nothing to walk on.
2. Elias stands still. A bridge of light grows.
3. He moves. The bridge breaks.
4. He stands still again. The bridge grows back.
5. Two glass shadows wait on a narrow span. He stays calm and deals with them.
6. He stands still near the Dragon. Its wind holds the bridge for 5 seconds.
7. Leeches chew on the bridge. He stands still and builds it again.
8. At the last bridge he steps once.
9. He holds still for **3 seconds**.
10. **Line 4** lights up in the sky.

**He learns:** calm builds. Rushing breaks.

---

## Chapter 10: flying (Line 5)

*Goal: do not hesitate.*

1. Elias finds the heat currents. They lift him up like warm air.
2. On his first ride he must not wait. If he waits, he falls to the last ledge.
3. The Dragon picks him up.
4. It carries him over a huge empty drop.
5. He rides the flow with no bridges. He can skip the enemies.
6. In the middle of the flight he rides one current just right. The Dragon answers with a long, low call.
7. He looks up and sees the Throne, far above.
8. He rides the last heat current without stopping.
9. He lands.
10. He holds still for **5 seconds**, with the wind pushing.
11. **Line 5** lights up in the sky.

**He learns:** after calm, action feels easy.

---

## Chapter 11: the summit (Line 6)

*Goal: stand still for 10 seconds.*

1. The Dragon sets him on the arrival ledge of a giant mountain.
2. The mountain is huge. It has five long flights of rock steps that zigzag up.
3. He climbs the first flight.
4. He rests on a flat landing. A thin bronze line crosses it.
5. He climbs the second, third, fourth and fifth flights.
6. The wind grows stronger with every flight.
7. He reaches the top crown. One column stands there.
8. The wind is huge. Everything says "Move!"
9. He stands still. He counts **10 seconds**.
10. If he moves, the count starts again.
11. Far off, a faint glass copy of Elias stands in the wind. It does not fight. It stands still too.
12. If he stays still, it fades.
13. **Line 6** lights up in the sky. All six lines burn.
14. The Fire Eye opens all the way.
15. The Dragon lands next to him.
16. He climbs on its back.

**He learns:** real power knows when to stop.

**A hidden thing:** at the foot of the Throne stair is a seam. Stand still and the first brazier lights early.

---

## Chapter 12: the Throne

*Goal: sit down. The world wakes up.*

1. The Dragon flies him over the whole world.
2. He sees every island he crossed.
3. The Dragon drops him at the foot of a long stair.
4. He climbs the stair by himself.
5. Six small dark bowls of fire stand on the sides, three on each side.
6. They light up as he passes.
7. At the top the Throne is giant, black and still.
8. Three huge bronze ribs curve over each side of its back.
9. It does nothing until he stops.
10. He stops.
11. He presses **E** to sit. This is his one real choice.
12. The Cinder melts into his suit.
13. Six cracks in the Throne light up, one by one, from the bottom to the top.
14. The Fire Eye opens all the way.
15. A ring of fire spreads over the whole world. It does not burn. It wakes things up.
16. Lanterns light, one after another.
17. The Eye slowly closes.
18. The screen fades to black.
19. **I-RULE01 complete.** No words. No boss. No score.

**If he found all seven hidden seams:** every lantern he lit burns gold, the Eye stays open to the end, and the six cracks glow gold.

---

## The short version (to tell in one breath)

1. Elias wakes up.
2. He learns to stand still.
3. He lights six fires, one by one.
4. Each fire needs him to stop.
5. The last one needs 10 whole seconds.
6. A Dragon flies him to the Throne.
7. He sits.
8. The whole world wakes up.

## Questions to ask the kid

1. What happens when Elias rushes?
2. What happens when he stands still?
3. Why did the door open?
4. Why is the last fire the hardest?
5. What would you do on the summit when the wind says "Move!"?
