# ENV-04 Large Plateau Island

**Units:** metres. Height 0 is the top of the island.

## 1. What this island is
1. A large floating island of dark rock, about 60 m wide. Sheer broken cliffs all around.
2. In the middle is one DEEP crater, about 30 m across and 18 m deep. Three broad broken ledges step down to a flat floor. Dim amber light glows in the cracks. It is the energy reservoir of Stage 2.
3. Six huge natural rock spires stand around the crater rim. They are scattered, not in a row. Heights: 55, 44, 34, 28, 22 and 16 m.
4. The two tallest stand at the east edge and lean toward each other. The gap between them is the way out, like a natural rock gate. The tallest is as tall as the island is wide.
5. The player comes in from the west, from the moving platforms (ENV-11). He lands on a flat west landing.
6. Three rough rock paths lead from the landing: north rim, south rim, and one straight to the crater edge. Only one way is aligned.
7. ONE ignition column stands on the east rim of the crater. This is the second column. It lights Line 2.
8. There are no towers, no buildings and no props. All the tall things are natural rock.
9. Stage 2 has no enemies. The trial is timing and stillness.

## 2. What the player does (the gamer loop)
Follow the steps in order. "Build" tells you what to make.

1. **Arrive and look.** Elias steps off the last moving platform onto the west landing. Jump is now unlocked. The glowing crater, the column and the huge gate spires are all in view. He can already see the goal.
   - **Build:** a landing with a low rock lip, a safe respawn point, a camera that frames the whole island.
2. **Read the paths.** Three ways lead forward. The north rim path looks easy and goes straight toward the column. The south rim path is long and quiet. The middle path goes down into the crater.
   - **Build:** three paths, each about 3 m wide, broken and uneven.
3. **Stand still, 1.5 s.** The Cinder glows toward the middle path. Thin bronze lines on the crater floor glow softly.
   - **Build:** a stillness check at the landing. No arrows, no text.
4. **The wrong paths crumble.** The north and south paths are cracked. If he steps on one, it trembles. After 2 seconds a slab drops. He falls to a crater ledge below. No damage. It is a soft reset.
   - **Build:** crack, tremble, drop. A respawn point on the top crater ledge.
5. **Cross the crater on moving slabs.** The middle path ends at the crater edge. Four slabs (ENV-11) drift across the crater, one after the other. He jumps, times it and dashes between them. The dash cooldown is longer here, so he must plan.
   - **Build:** four moving slabs across the crater with timed cycles. Small tremors when they pass.
6. **The reservoir.** On the crater floor he stands still. Amber light climbs the three ledges, one ring at a time. His energy comes back. This is the lesson: energy returns, do not waste it.
   - **Build:** an alignment check on the floor. Amber light that climbs the ledges. Energy refills while he is still.
7. **Climb out to the column.** He climbs the east ledges (broken ramps) to the east rim. The column stands there, dark and dry, below the two giant spires.
   - **Build:** broken ramps up the east ledges. The column with a hold trigger in front of it.
8. **Hold for 3 seconds.** He faces the right heading and holds still for 3 seconds while the slabs drift below. This is the pressure, with no enemies. Fire catches in the bowl on top. The bronze lines glow. Line 2 ignites.
   - **Build:** a 3 s hold trigger. Fire in the bowl. A glow along the bronze lines. The second bar of the six lines lights.
9. **The gate opens.** The gap between the two spires brightens. A short stone bridge leads out. Beyond it, the way goes on toward Stage 3.
   - **Build:** a light change in the gap. The bridge end with one bronze socket post. A trigger to load the next place.

## 4. What the player learns here
1. Energy comes back. He does not have to waste it.
2. The obvious way is not always the right way. Standing still shows the right way.
3. Timing is the trial. Nothing attacks him.

## 6. Rock rules
1. The rock is the same dark black-brown fractured rock as ENV-01, 02 and 03.
2. The spires are thick at the base and taper. They are crooked and ragged, with deep vertical cracks and broken tops. Each leans at a different angle. They are NOT in a row, NOT in a fan, NOT like fingers, NOT blocks.
3. The crater is an irregular oval. Not round, not neat rings, no wedding-cake tiers.
4. Dry and dead. No grass, no trees, no moss.
5. Nothing is built except the bronze lines, the one column and one bridge end.
6. No towers, no walls, no windows, no arches, no stairs.

### No props
1. The island has no props. Do not add anything without asking.

## 7. Sounds and mood
1. Wind drone, medium. Softer inside the crater.
2. A soft timing pulse when the slabs are near.
3. A resonance tone rises when he is aligned and still.
4. When the column lights: a deep whoosh, then soft crackling fire. The bronze lines hum.
5. The mood is grand and watchful. The light is warm gold.

## 8. Enemies
1. None. Combat is off in Stage 2.
2. The danger is the cracked paths and the moving slabs. A fall is a soft reset.

## 9. How it connects
1. Order of play: ENV-03, then the moving platforms (ENV-11), then **ENV-04**, then ENV-05, then ENV-06.
2. Height here is about +10 m above ENV-01. The Starting Shelf is now visible far below.
3. **In (west).** The last of 4 moving platforms (ENV-11) brings the player to the flat west landing, about 10 m wide. The platform has an anchor socket (STR-09) at each end of its rail. The way back stays open.
4. **Out (east).** The rock gate: a short stone bridge (STR-06, about 12 m) between the two tallest spires. It leads out to ENV-05.
5. After the bridge there are 3 brittle slabs (ENV-12). They climb from +11 m to +18 m. The player lands on the ENV-05 causeway at +18 m. See docs/23, section 9, and Sheet C.
6. Inside the island: the landing, then 3 paths (north rim, south rim, crater edge), then the crater and the column on the east rim, then the rock gate. Only the crater path is aligned.
7. The player can skip the reservoir but cannot skip the column. He needs the column lit to pass.
8. A fall inside the crater is a soft reset onto a crater ledge. A fall from the slabs is a soft reset to the last anchor.

## 11. Notes for builders
1. The crater is the energy reservoir. It must read as a dry, ancient place that fills with amber light, never with water.
2. Which path is the aligned one is a design choice. The blueprint uses the middle path. You may change it.
3. The Cinder is the hint. Do not use arrows or text.
4. Make the spires very large next to the island. The two gate spires are the landmark you see from far away.
5. The pictures may differ a little from this page. Developers decide.

## 12. Known picture differences (developers decide)
1. The pictures show a shallow round pit with stone steps, about 6 m deep. The blueprint says an irregular oval crater, 18 m deep, 3 broken ledges and NO stairs. For the crater, use the blueprint pictures. Build ledges and broken ramps, not stairs.
2. The pictures show 5 or 6 spires in a row along the north rim. The blueprint says six spires scattered around the whole rim, not in a row.
3. The back picture, the top picture and detail 4 show extra small braziers and a stone path through the gate. The blueprint has exactly ONE column and one short bridge end. Build only one column.
4. The pictures show the bridge end on the west side in the back view. The bridge is on the east. The top picture is the reference.
5. For everything else, the sheet and the blueprint pictures win.
