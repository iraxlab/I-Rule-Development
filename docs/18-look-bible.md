## Ideas to make it more interesting (PROPOSED, need owner approval)
- **Six lines as six elements:** the lines run through the whole space as ember, molten bronze, wind, lightning, liquid fire, white-gold light (SKY-10). Each stage's pylons and floor inlays use that stage's element.
- **Pylon puzzle:** a pylon lights one arm each time Elias stands still on its disc. Light all six arms to open the next rung bridge.
- **Leeches under long bridges:** they cling to the underside and strike up through gaps only when Elias is mid-bridge. A held stillness ring pushes them back (stillness rings, VFX-03).
- **Eye reacts to the climb:** closed and dormant at the start, opens one stage at a time, burning when the Throne is reached (the four Eye states).
- **Brazier checkpoints:** standing bronze braziers (STR-30) light when Elias passes and act as rest points.
- **Dragon mount:** Elias must wake the Fire Dragon and ride it. Canon says the dragon lands at 78:00 and carries him to the foot of the Throne stairs; the sample images also show a ride in Stage 5. Decide which.

## Update: not a toy, and the door must be locked, then unlocked, then entered
- **First ENV-01 sheet looked like a toy** (small clay-like chunk, tidy wedding-cake towers, matching green domes, even flat colours). - **Door states.** ENV-01 shows the door LOCKED (two thick chains wound across both leaves, one huge closed padlock, light through the gap). ## Update: natural, not staged
The second ENV-01 sheet was still too staged: the door stood alone like a prop and the towers were lined up beside it on a neat slab. Rules now in every island and structure prompt: nothing stands alone; every structure is grown out of, carved into or half-buried in the rock, with scree, rubble and fallen blocks at its base; irregular outlines, uneven ground, off-centre overlapping forms, no neat rows, no mirror symmetry, one obvious focal point.
ENV-01 is rebuilt on that: a ragged, prow-shaped shelf (not a rectangle) with broken rim lips; the far end rises into a huge black rock cliff with the door cut into its face; two natural rock pillars of the same dark rock (60 m and a leaning 36 m, NOT built towers, see docs/19) grow out of the cliff above the door. The playthrough door blocks and the door states (STR-34, STR-35, ENV-01 details 6 and 7) use the same cliff-door setting.

## Update: where Elias appears and the way to the door
The last ENV-01 sheet had no clear place to start and no clear way to the door. ENV-01 now has a fixed layout, shown in the diagram below (attach it when generating ENV-01, and every ENV-01 prompt says to follow it):

(The early diagram was replaced by the exact blueprint in docs/19.)

1. **Spawn terrace** at the near, low end: a flat oval apron of huge paving slabs about 12 m across, the bronze arrival disc at its centre, a low broken rock lip behind it, open and uncluttered. Elias appears here, standing on the disc, facing the door.
2. **The road:** one broad worn paving road with a thin bronze inlay line, the only clear route, rubble cleared to its sides.
3. **Ravine and the Beam:** the level bronze beam crosses the ravine in the middle of the road.
4. **Door forecourt:** a flat cleared plaza about 12 m wide with three broad steps up to the door threshold.
5. **The door**, locked, cut into the cliff face, with the ruined towers growing out of the cliff above. Elias unlocks it (PROPOSED: by standing still) and walks in.

ENV-01 now has eight detail prompts, including Spawn terrace (detail 3) and Forecourt and steps (detail 8). The first frame of the playthrough starts on the disc, and the locked-door frame starts at the foot of the forecourt steps.
