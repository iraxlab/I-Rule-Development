# ENV-09 Drifting Fragment Cluster: Blueprint (Stage 5, Flying)

Sheets: [A top](../art/guide/env09-blueprint-A-top.png) · [B side](../art/guide/env09-blueprint-B-side.png) · [C connection](../art/guide/env09-blueprint-C-connection.png)

## 1. What it is
The flight field. Seven separate fragments of one shattered mountain float at +50 to +65 m in golden sky above a sea of cloud. About 390 m from the launch terrace to the debris rocks. **No bridges, no ropes, no stairs.** Heat, the Dragon and nerve are the only ways across. Natural dark black-brown fractured rock only: no towers, arches, spheres, trees or grass.

## 2. The fragments (west to east, top view)

| 1 | Launch Terrace | 36 m | +50 | Arrival. Calm pocket. Learn the heat current |
| 2 | First Rise | 28 m | +56 | First ride: commit |

Also: two brittle slabs south of the Great Fragment (collapse), and the debris rocks (ENV-10) at the east end.

## 3. Heat currents
Six, drawn only as shimmer with a faint amber glow at the base. 1 (terrace to First Rise); 2, 3, 4 (centre, north, south lanes around the Great Fragment); 5 (to the Ignition Ledge); 6 (the last one, from the debris rocks, lifts him about 225 m to the Summit foot at +290 m). He must not stop in it.

## 4. Dragon carry
Between the First Rise and the Great Fragment, a 48 m void. Aligned flight is clean and silent; forcing it adds turbulence.

## 5. Failure and rest
A hesitation fall is caught by the Cinder and resets him to the previous launch ledge. Four calm pockets (4.5 m rings) are the rest points.

## 6. Order of play
ENV-08 launch lip, Launch Terrace, First Rise, Dragon carry, Great Fragment, one of three lanes, Mid Fragment, Reveal Ledge, Ignition Ledge (hold 5 s, Line 5), debris rocks, last heat current, ENV-07.

## 7. Connection
In: warm air from the ENV-08 launch lip (+50 to +65 m). Out: last heat current to the ENV-07 arrival ledge (+290 m).
