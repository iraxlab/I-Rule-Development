# Funding and submission

**Target program:** Epic MegaGrants (projects built in Unreal Engine). Evaluation criteria: quality, community benefit, viability.

**Ask:** $25,000-$50,000 to take the project from concept to a first playable Stage 1 vertical slice.

## Proposed use of funds
| Share | Use |
|---|---|
| ~40% | Lead gameplay programmer (Unreal, C++ / Blueprints): player controller, stillness state machine, line ignition |
| ~25% | Environment / technical art for the Stage 1 blockout (island, six ladder bars, lantern prefabs, fire ignition VFX) |
| ~15% | Audio: adaptive wind / harmonic layers tied to stillness and ignition |
| ~10% | Software, asset licences and hardware |
| ~10% | Contingency and playtesting for the Stage 1 slice |

## Elevator pitch (224 characters)
> An action-adventure where power comes from restraint, not force. Players ignite six ancient hexagram lines by mastering stillness and timing, inspired by the I Ching, in a mythic sky-world with no combat spam, no HUD, no rush.

## Unique features
- **Stillness as a core progression mechanic.**
- **Six-line ladder always visible in the sky.**
- **No HUD, no tutorial text**: the world teaches through audio, light and environmental response.
- **Fire as a temporary, alignment-gated resource.**
- **A non-violent climax**: activation (a Ring of Fire) instead of a boss.
