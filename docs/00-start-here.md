# Start here

Pick your role, read in this order, and you will know enough to work without asking. Each path is about 30-45 minutes.

## Everyone (10 minutes)
1. [README](../README.md): the game in 30 seconds.
1. [How it plays](how-it-plays.md): what the player actually does.
2. [Vision and pillars](01-vision-and-pillars.md): seven rules every decision is tested against.
3. [Canon and open decisions](13-canon-and-open-decisions.md): what you may never change, and what is still open.

## Gameplay programmer
1. [Core systems](06-core-systems.md): the hidden Creative Energy Cycle, stillness, fire, combat, traversal.
2. [Unreal architecture](10-unreal-architecture.md): classes, data assets, input.
3. [Stages](stages/README.md): thresholds, hold times and failure loops you must implement.
4. [Production plan](11-production-plan.md): build order. **Movement feel is gate 1.**

## Level and environment designer

3. [Stages](stages/README.md): beat plans per stage.
4. [Art direction](12-art-direction.md): materials and the forbidden list.
