# ENV-10 Debris Rocks: Blueprint (Stage 5, the last steps)

Sheets: [A top](../art/guide/env10-blueprint-A-top.png) · [B side](../art/guide/env10-blueprint-B-side.png) · [C the four rock types](../art/guide/env10-blueprint-C-kit.png)

## 1. What it is
Six small floating rocks at the far east end of the flight field ([doc 35](35-env09-blueprint.md)), between the Ignition Ledge and the last heat current. They are the last steps of Stage 5: Elias runs across them and into the current that carries him 225 m up to the Summit foot. Natural dark rock only: no bronze, carving, stairs or props.

## 2. The four rock types
| Type | Width | Used as | Look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Squat boulder | 4.4 to 6.8 m | R1, R5 | Angular, flat top, thick root |
| Split slab | 6 m | R2 | Tall slab with one vertical split |
| Long wedge | 4 to 5 m wide, 8 m long | R3, R6 | One sharp ridge |
| Block cluster | 3.4 m | R4 | Three broken blocks pressed together |
All dark black-brown fractured rock like the Starting Shelf Island. A thin amber glow in one crack only.

## 4. How it plays
After Line 5 lights he steps off the ledge onto R1, runs R2, R3, R4, feels the air shimmer and enters the last current. He must not stop. There is no fall here: a hesitation sends him back to R1 to try again. R5 and R6 are optional side rocks for the curious.

## 5. Connection
In: the Ignition Ledge (ENV-09, +65 m). Out: the last heat current to the ENV-07 arrival ledge (+290 m).
