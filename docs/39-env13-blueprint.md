# ENV-13 Windbreak Ridge: Blueprint (Stage 1)

Sheets: [A top view](../art/guide/env13-blueprint-A-top.png) · [B side and wind shadow](../art/guide/env13-blueprint-B-side.png) · [C build notes](../art/guide/env13-blueprint-C-ridge.png)

> **PROPOSED, not locked.** The tracker had only a short line for this asset. Its old description (a tiny ruined stair and bronze brackets) broke the "natural rock only" canon. This page redesigns it and gives it a place. The producer decides.

## 1. What it is
One free-floating curved blade of dark black-brown rock, like a shark fin or a scimitar: 25 m long, 8 m tall, foot 5 m thick, thin notched crest 0.5 m. It rises from a rough broken shoulder about 6 m wide, with a short thick root about 5 m deep. It is a landmark and a wind shelter. **It is not walkable.** No stair, no bronze brackets, no Six-Line Pylon, no door, no props.

## 2. Shape
The long upper edge is one smooth sweeping curve leaning slightly east, with three or four small irregular notches. The north (windward) face is carved by wind into long vertical flutes and grooves. The south (sheltered) face is plainer and smoother. Same rock as ENV-01 to ENV-12.

## 3. Place and use (Stage 1)
It floats 5.5 m north of the 12 m plank bridge between ENV-02 and ENV-03, centred on the bridge, with its foot level with the bridge. Wind comes from the north, so the blade stands between the wind and the bridge.
- Outside its shadow the wind bed is loud (0.75). In the lee it drops to about 0.15 over 1 s. The calm air is about 12 m long.
- A calm window on the bridge (x 4 to 8) is the **first alignment pocket** of Stage 1 (beat 04:20 to 05:00, [stage 1](stages/stage-1-lurking.md)). Standing still 2 s there clears the sound a little. Rushing gives no calm.
- The ENV-03 massif shelters the far end of the bridge, so the calm carries on into the hollow, where the column is.
- It must read from the ENV-02 spire as a huge shape between Elias and the east.

## 4. Why
Stage 1 asks him to stop and find calm. This gives the calm pocket a physical cause (a rock that blocks the wind) and a reason to walk towards the east, with no UI and no rules shown.
