# ENV-12 Brittle Slab: Blueprint (Stage 3 way in)

Sheets: [A top view](../art/guide/env12-blueprint-A-top.png) · [B side and timing](../art/guide/env12-blueprint-B-side.png) · [C the slab](../art/guide/env12-blueprint-C-slab.png)

## 1. What it is
One thin slab of layered natural rock, used three times between the ENV-04 rock gate and the ENV-05 causeway ([doc 22](22-env04-blueprint.md), [doc 23](23-env05-blueprint.md)). It cracks 2 s after he first stands on it and falls. It is the only gap on the way in that he can fall from. No bronze, no carving, no machinery: only dark black-brown rock and the amber light in its cracks.

## 2. The slab
6 m long, 4.5 m wide, about 1 m thick: four or five thin flat layers stacked like slate, peeling apart at the edges, with a short thin root about 1.5 m deep. The top is flat but tilted about 18 degrees (2 m over 6 m) so the east edge is higher. A web of hairline cracks covers the top. A few small flakes already drift off the rim.

## 3. Placement (blueprint x, west to east)
| Slab | x range | Side offset | Low edge | High edge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | 3 to 9 | 0 | +11.0 | +13.0 |
| B2 | 13 to 19 | +1.5 | +13.5 | +15.5 |
| B3 | 23.5 to 29.5 | -1.0 | +16.0 | +18.0 |
The stone bridge from the rock gate ends at x 0, +11 m. The ENV-05 west tongue starts at x 32.5, +18 m. Gaps: 3 m, 4 m, 4.5 m, 3 m. Each hop up is only 0.5 m, which fits the 1.6 m jump ([doc 06](06-core-systems.md)). The 7 m climb is carried by the slope of the slabs, not by the hops. The slabs are offset sideways so the way is never a straight row.

## 4. Timing (time from his first foot)
0 s hairlines glow under his foot. 1 s low creak. 1.5 s cracks widen, flakes fall. 2 s the layers split and fall, top sheet first. Anyone on it falls: soft reset to the last anchor (the bridge end). The count never stops once started. The slab re-forms by itself 8 s after it falls, in silence.

## 5. Why it works
Crossing a 6 m slab takes about 1 s, so the 2 s count is fair but never allows standing still. This teaches the opposite of Stillness on purpose: here he must keep moving. The next stop, ENV-05, is the long breath.
